About the Project

A smarter lens on everyday habits

MindScope AI is a machine learning project that studies how digital behaviour, academic routines, sleep, movement, and stress relate to a mental health score. You provide a few details, the trained model processes them through a FastAPI service, and you receive a model-generated score.

AI-Powered Prediction

A trained regression model returns a predicted score directly from your inputs, with no hardcoded values.

Digital Lifestyle Analysis

Platform choice, purpose of use, daily screen time, and phone unlocks describe your digital footprint.

Sleep Pattern Assessment

Nightly sleep duration is one of the key signals the model considers alongside other habits.

Academic Lifestyle Insights

Academic level and daily study hours add context about workload and routine.

Physical Activity Tracking

Daily physical activity hours capture how much movement is part of your day.

Stress Level Analysis

Self-reported stress, from Low to Very High, is combined with the rest of your profile.
